3 quick fixes for Stop 4 Nails

I looked at stop4nailsrenton.com on a phone, the way most of your customers see it. Your prices, hours, and photos are all there, which is great. Here are 3 small changes that could bring in more bookings. You or your web person can do each one in a few minutes.

1Put "Book Here" where people can see it

What I saw: On a phone, the Book Here button only shows up after you tap the ☰ menu. It's not on the welcome screen or on the long Services page.

Easy fix: In the Wix mobile editor, add a big "Book Now" button (linked to your Square booking) under "Welcome" and at the top and bottom of the Services page.

2Let customers text you

What I saw: Your site lists a phone number to call, but there's no way to send you a text.

Easy fix: Add a "Text us" button next to the phone number, so people can ask a quick question without calling while you're busy with a client.

3Make "See Directions" give directions

What I saw: "See Directions" opens the Contact page, which has no map. The address isn't tappable, and on a phone it gets cut off at the edge of the screen.

Easy fix: Link the address and the "See Directions" button to Google Maps, add a small map to the Contact page, and fix the address so it fits on the screen.

No strings attached. It's your site, and these are yours to use however you like. I'm happy to point to the exact spots if that helps.
